Leap Year

A leap year (in the Gregorian calendar) occurs: In every year that is evenly divisible by 4. Unless the year is evenly divisible by 100, in which case it's only a leap year if the year is also evenly divisible by 400. Some examples: 1997 was not a leap year as it's not divisible by 4. 1900 was not a leap year as it's not divisible by 400. 2000 was a leap year!
